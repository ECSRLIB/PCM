About the data

How the library was put together, and how to use it responsibly.

What's inside

We merged several literature tables and a commercial-product compilation into one record per material, then added well-established handbook values. Each record keeps all its reported values and their sources.

  • Organic: paraffins, fatty acids, esters, alcohols, sugar alcohols, PEGs
  • Inorganic: salt hydrates, molten salts, metals and alloys
  • Eutectics and solid–solid PCMs
  • Commercial products from Rubitherm, PureTemp, Croda, Climator, Axiotherm, PLUSS, PCM Products and others

Quality checks

Every record passed automatic checks. Rows were flagged or corrected when they had:

  • a minus sign lost from a sub-zero melting point
  • density in g/cm³ instead of kg/m³
  • values in the wrong column (for example, density entered as conductivity)
  • impossible values, such as a negative density

Values vary between sources because of sample purity and DSC method. Confirm critical design values against the original reference.

Download & reuse

The whole library is free to download for research and teaching.
